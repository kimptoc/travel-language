📇 Quick Reference

🇫🇷 French Foundation: A 9-Week Course

Practical survival + courtesy French, structured the same way as the Tokyo and Korean plans — but since French uses the Latin alphabet, the reading barrier is much lower, so this plan shifts the early weeks to pronunciation and core grammar instead. Leans toward Paris-relevant situations throughout.

Commitment: ~15–20 minutes/day, 9 weeks.

⭐ Highest priority: Week 5

If you're vegetarian, Week 5 (Restaurants & Dietary Requirements) matters most — plus keep the Quick Reference card on your phone for the trip itself.

The 9 weeks

Week 1

Vowel Sounds & Silent Letters

Nasal vowels (on, an, in, un) and why final consonants are usually silent — this is where most self-taught learners go wrong.

 Week 2

Liaison & Rhythm

Linking words together (les_amis, vous_êtes) and basic stress/rhythm patterns.

 Week 3

Courtesy & Core Phrases

Bonjour/bonsoir, excusez-moi, merci, oui/non, and a basic self-introduction.

 Week 4

Numbers, Time & Money

Numbers 1–100, including the soixante-dix / quatre-vingts quirks, and reading euro prices aloud.

 Week 5 · High Priority

Restaurants & Dietary Requirements

Vegetarian phrases, asking what's in a dish, and the bouillon (stock) trap hiding in "vegetable" soups.

 Week 6

Hotel & Daily Logistics

Check-in/out, lost keys, luggage, taxis and wifi.

 Week 7

Transport & Directions

Paris Métro stations, Navigo top-ups, and right/left/straight ahead.

 Week 8

Small Talk & Basic Grammar

être / avoir, j'aime, je voudrais, and simple past marker basics.

 Week 9

Consolidation & Simulation

Re-run earlier weeks as flashcards and simulate real scenarios end-to-end.

Tools to pair with this plan

  • Anki — spaced repetition for vocab and verb conjugations.
  • Forvo — native pronunciation audio for tricky words.
  • Google Translate camera mode — backup for menus, not a substitute for the above.
