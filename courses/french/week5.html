⚠️ The bouillon (stock) trap

French cuisine's hidden trap: many "vegetable" soups and sauces use meat or fish stock (bouillon/fond) as a base even when the dish looks vegetarian. Worth asking specifically: Le bouillon est à base de viande ou de poisson ? (is the stock meat- or fish-based?)
