📇 Quick Reference

🇰🇷 Korean Foundation: A 9-Week Course

Practical survival + courtesy Korean, structured the same way as the Tokyo Japanese plan — reading foundations first, then situational phrases building up to independent basic conversation.

Commitment: ~15–20 minutes/day, 9 weeks.

⭐ Highest priority: Week 5

If you're vegetarian, Week 5 (Restaurants & Dietary Requirements) matters most — plus keep the Quick Reference card on your phone for the trip itself.

The 9 weeks

Week 1

Hangul: Consonants & Vowels

14 basic consonants + 10 basic vowels. Korea's big advantage: a phonetic alphabet you can master in days.

 Week 2

Syllable Blocks & Batchim

Double consonants, combined syllable blocks, and batchim (final consonants) — the last pieces to read any word.

 Week 3

Courtesy & Core Phrases

Greetings, 죄송합니다/미안합니다, thank-yous, and a basic self-introduction.

 Week 4

Numbers, Time & Money

Korea's two number systems (Sino-Korean and Native Korean) and reading won prices.

 Week 5 · High Priority

Restaurants & Dietary Requirements

Vegetarian phrases, asking what's in a dish, and the fish sauce (액젓) trap hiding in banchan and kimchi.

 Week 6

Hotel & Daily Logistics

Check-in/out, lost keys, luggage, taxis and wifi.

 Week 7

Transport & Directions

Stations, subways, T-money card top-ups, and right/left/straight.

 Week 8

Small Talk & Basic Grammar

Light grammar patterns to make sentences more flexible for conversation.

 Week 9

Consolidation & Simulation

Re-run earlier weeks as flashcards and simulate real scenarios end-to-end.

Tools to pair with this plan

  • Anki — spaced repetition for Hangul and vocab (this site's flashcards work well alongside it).
  • Naver Dictionary app — better than Google Translate for Korean, includes example sentences.
  • Google Translate camera mode — backup for menus, not a substitute for the above.
