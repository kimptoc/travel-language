⚠️ The fish sauce (액젓) trap

Many banchan (side dishes) and kimchi use fish sauce even when the main dish looks vegetarian — the Korean equivalent of Japan's dashi trap. Worth asking 이거 액젓 들어있어요? (igeo aekjeot deureoisseoyo — does this have fish sauce in it?) specifically.
