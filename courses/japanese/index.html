📇 Quick Reference

🇯🇵 Tokyo Foundation: A 9-Week Japanese Course

Practical survival + courtesy Japanese for a two-week Tokyo trip (Conrad Tokyo, Daigo dinner, general travel) — not JLPT study. Built around real situations you'll hit, including explaining vegetarian dietary requirements.

Commitment: ~15–20 minutes/day, 9 weeks (mid-July → mid-September).

⭐ Highest priority: Week 5

You're strictly vegetarian and dining at Daigo. Week 5 (Restaurants & Dietary Requirements) matters most — plus keep the Quick Reference card on your phone for the trip itself.

The 9 weeks

Week 1

Hiragana

46 base characters + dakuten/handakuten (が, ぱ, etc). Flashcards + writing practice.

 Week 2

Katakana

Used for foreign loanwords — menus and hotel terms like メニュー, ホテル, ロビー.

 Week 3

Courtesy & Core Phrases

Greetings, すみません, thank-yous, and a basic self-introduction.

 Week 4

Numbers, Time & Money

Counting, telling time, and asking prices — including reading yen amounts.

 Week 5 · High Priority

Restaurants & Dietary Requirements

Vegetarian phrases, asking what's in a dish, and the dashi (fish stock) trap — critical for Daigo.

 Week 6

Hotel & Daily Logistics

Check-in/out, lost keys, luggage, taxis and wifi — Conrad Tokyo context.

 Week 7

Transport & Directions

Stations, trains, Suica/IC card top-ups, and left/right/straight.

 Week 8

Small Talk & Basic Grammar

Light grammar patterns to make sentences more flexible for conversation.

 Week 9

Consolidation & Simulation

Re-run earlier weeks as flashcards and simulate real Tokyo scenarios end-to-end.

Tools to pair with this plan

  • Anki — spaced repetition for hiragana/katakana and vocab (this site's flashcards work well alongside it).
  • NHK Easy Japanese News — light reading practice once hiragana is solid.
  • Google Translate camera mode — backup for menus, not a substitute for the above.
