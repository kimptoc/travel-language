⚠️ The dashi trap

Dashi (fish stock) is the hidden trap in Japanese vegetarian dining — worth having 出汁は魚ですか (dashi wa sakana desu ka — is the dashi made from fish?) ready, even at a place like Daigo where your dietary notes are already on file.
